What We Fix

Common Cooker Faults We Repair in Nairobi

Whatever the fault, our technicians have the parts and expertise for professional Cooker Repair in Nairobi on the same day.

1

Burner Won't Ignite

Clogged burner ports, faulty spark electrodes, damaged ignition switches or gas supply issues preventing ignition. We clean, adjust or replace components to restore reliable lighting.

2

Gas Leakage

Emergency gas leak detection and repair. We locate and seal leaks in gas lines, valves, regulator hoses and connections and conduct a full safety test before sign-off.

3

Oven Not Heating

Faulty heating elements, broken thermostats, defective control boards and gas valve problems fixed with calibrated tools and genuine parts for accurate temperature control.

4

Yellow or Orange Flames

Incomplete combustion caused by dirty burners or incorrect air-to-gas ratios. We clean burner components and tune air mixture for safe blue flames.

5

Thermostat Malfunction

Inaccurate temperature readings causing over- or under-cooking. We calibrate or replace thermostats and temperature sensors for precise cooking control.

6

Broken Control Knobs or Panel

Damaged knobs, unresponsive touch panels, blank displays and error codes repaired. We source replacement knobs and control boards for all brands.

7

Door Seal & Hinge Failure

Worn door gaskets and broken hinges cause heat loss, slow cooking and safety hazards. We replace seals and realign doors on ovens and cookers of all makes.

8

Induction Error Codes

Error codes E0, E1, E2, F1 and others on induction hobs diagnosed and resolved. We fix coil failures, thermistor faults and control PCB issues on all induction brands.

9

Continuous Clicking

A cooker that keeps clicking after lighting is annoying and can indicate moisture or a faulty spark module. We dry out components and replace igniters to stop unwanted clicking.

10

Fan Oven Not Circulating

Fan motor failures cause hot and cold spots in fan-assisted ovens. We replace fan motors, fan blades and thermal fuses to restore even heat distribution.

11

Electrical Wiring Faults

Tripped breakers, burnt wiring, faulty heating elements and loose connections repaired to full electrical safety standards. All electrical Cooker Repair in Nairobi is tested before handover.

12

Timer & Self-Clean Faults

Digital timers that won't count down, clocks that reset and self-cleaning cycles that fail to complete — we repair or replace timing mechanisms and door lock assemblies.
